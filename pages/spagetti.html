Sylo's Spaghetti Bolognese


Everyone needs a Spaghetti bolognses recipe that tastes great, not matter how simple or complex it is to make
thats why I have compiled in my opinion THE GREATEST SPAGHETTI BOLOGNESE RECIPE you can find on the internet

Ingredients:

  • 2 tbsp of olive oil
  • 400g of beef mince
  • 1 finely chopped onion
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 40g of dried spaghetti
  • 2 x 400g tins of chopped tomatoes
  • 400ml of stock (Usually just a stock cube, ideally beef)

Method:

  1. Heat a large saucepan over a medium heat. Add a tablespoon of olive oil. Once hot, add the beef mince and a pinch of salt and pepper. Cook the mince until well browned over a medium-high heat (don't rush this step but be careful not to burn the mince. It needs to be a dark brown colour with some crispy caramelised bits). Once browned, transfer the mince to a bowl and set aside.

  2. Add another tablespoon of oil to the saucepan you browned the mince in and turn the heat to medium. Add the onions and a pinch of salt and fry gently for 5‐6 minutes, or until soft and translucent. Add the garlic and cook for another 2 minutes. Add the grated carrot. Return the mince (and any juices in the bowl) back to the saucepan.

  3. Add the tomatoes and stir well to mix. Pour in the stock and bring to a simmer. Reduce the heat and simmer gently for 45 minutes, or until the sauce is thick and rich. Taste and adjust the seasoning as necessary.

  4. When ready to cook the spaghetti, heat a large saucepan of water and add a pinch of salt. Cook according to the packet instructions.

  5. If your bolognese sauce is too thick, stir in 1‐2 tablespoons of the starchy pasta water to loosen.

  6. Once the spaghetti is cooked through, drain and add the pasta to the pan with the bolognese sauce. Mix well and serve.
